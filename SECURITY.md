# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report security problems privately through
[GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/c0ldplasma/coldchat/security/advisories/new),
not in a public issue. Include what an attacker could do, the steps to reproduce it, and the app version and build
(shown at the bottom of Settings).

You'll get an answer within a week. Once a fix is released, the advisory is published, crediting you if you like.

## Supported versions

Only the latest release on the App Store and Google Play gets security fixes.

## Scope

ColdChat runs on your phone and talks only to Twitch and the emote, badge and upload services listed in the
[privacy policy](https://c0ldplasma.github.io/coldchat/privacy.html). In scope are problems in the app itself, for
example a way to read or leak your Twitch login, or a message or emote that makes the app do something it shouldn't.
Problems in Twitch or the other services themselves have to be reported to them.
