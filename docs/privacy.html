Privacy Policy

ColdChat for Android and iOS

Support Privacy Policy Terms of Service Source code

Last updated: October 24, 2026

In short: ColdChat has no servers and no accounts of its own. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting. The developer receives no data about you. Your settings and logins stay on your device, and the app talks directly to Twitch and the chat services listed below.

Who is responsible

ColdChat is a free, open-source hobby project by C0ldPlasma (github.com/c0ldplasma). For questions about this policy, open an issue in the issue tracker.

Data stored on your device

ColdChat stores the following only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your Twitch logins: the access token Twitch issues, your user name and user ID.
  • The channels you added, your settings, custom commands, message and user triggers.
  • Cached emote and badge lists, and which mentions you have already seen.
  • A list of the files you uploaded: their links, deletion links and upload times.

This data is not sent anywhere except as described below. Removing an account in the app deletes its login; uninstalling the app deletes all of it.

Services ColdChat connects to

To show and send chat, ColdChat connects directly from your device to the services below. Like any internet service, each one receives your IP address and the requests needed for the feature. Their own privacy policies apply to what they do with it.

Permissions

  • Camera and photo library: only when you choose to take or attach a photo. Nothing is uploaded unless you send it.
  • Location: ColdChat never uses your location. iOS requires a description for it only because the built-in web view, used for signing in, could ask for it.

Children

ColdChat is not directed at children. Twitch requires its users to be at least 13 years old.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete a login by removing the account in the app; delete everything by uninstalling it.
  • Revoke ColdChat's access to your Twitch account under Twitch → Settings → Connections.
  • The developer holds no personal data about you. For data held by Twitch or the other services above, including requests under the GDPR, contact those services directly.
